**Consumer Behavior Analysis: Garnier Face Wash**

**Project Overview:**

This project explores consumer behavior related to Garnier Face Wash through a survey conducted to gauge customer satisfaction and loyalty. The analysis was performed using Power BI, and the findings were presented via PowerPoint.

**My Contributions:**

Survey Creation:
Designed and distributed a survey using Google Forms to collect data on customer satisfaction, loyalty, and preferences for Garnier Face Wash.

Data Analysis in Power BI:
Analyzed the collected survey data using Power BI, identifying key metrics such as:

85% satisfaction rate among users.
56% loyalty rate, driven by product quality. Created visualizations to highlight important trends and insights.
Presentation in PowerPoint:
Compiled the insights and visualizations into a PowerPoint presentation, summarizing the key findings and conclusions about consumer behavior.

**Technologies Used:**

Google Forms: For designing and conducting the consumer survey.
Power BI: For data analysis and visualization of survey results.
PowerPoint: For presenting the conclusions and insights.


**Outcome:**

Discovered a high satisfaction rate (85%) and moderate loyalty (56%) among Garnier Face Wash users.
Demonstrated the importance of product quality in driving customer loyalty.
Gained valuable experience in conducting surveys, analyzing consumer data, and presenting insights effectively.
